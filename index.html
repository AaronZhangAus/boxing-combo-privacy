Privacy Policy

Boxing Combo Creator
Effective Date: 18 November 2026

1. Introduction

Boxing Combo Creator ("the App") is an Android application designed to help users practise boxing combinations through generated training sequences and spoken audio instructions.

We respect your privacy and aim to explain clearly how the App and the service providers used for purchases handle information.

2. Information Collection

The App does not require registration or the creation of an app user account.

We do not operate a server that receives or stores your saved workout content. The App does not send saved workout names, combinations or generated workout audio to the developer, Google Play or RevenueCat.

The App does not request your name or email address for normal use and does not access your precise location, contacts, photos or microphone. Purchase-related and technical information is processed as described in sections 6 and 7.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include so that we can respond. Please do not send payment-card details, passwords or other sensitive credentials.

3. Audio Instructions

Boxing Combo Creator uses pre-generated audio files bundled with the application to provide spoken boxing instructions. Workout audio is assembled and played locally on your device.

The App does not record your voice or transmit microphone recordings or training instructions to an online text-to-speech service. Core workout creation and playback work offline once the required app assets are available. Purchasing, restoring purchases and refreshing purchase status require an internet connection.

4. Analytics and Advertising

The App does not display advertisements or integrate advertising tracking services. It does not use a separate general-purpose usage-analytics or crash-reporting service.

RevenueCat processes purchase-related information and associated technical information to support purchase validation, Pro access, restoration and purchase reporting, as described below.

5. Local Device Storage

The App stores information in app-private storage on your device to support its functionality. This includes saved workout names, boxing combinations, workout settings and dates, generated workout audio, and app preferences. Purchase-management components may also cache purchase and entitlement status locally.

You can delete saved workouts using the App's saved-workout controls. You can remove app data through Android's application storage settings or by uninstalling the App, subject to your device's backup and restore settings. Clearing app data or uninstalling may permanently remove saved workouts.

6. In-App Purchases, Google Play and RevenueCat

Boxing Combo Creator is free to download and offers an optional one-time in-app purchase to unlock Pro features. The price is displayed by Google Play before you confirm a purchase.

We use Google Play to process payments and RevenueCat to validate purchases, manage Pro access and restore purchases. These services may process:

  • Product identifiers, transaction records, purchase tokens and purchase history.
  • An anonymous app user identifier used by RevenueCat.
  • Purchase, refund and entitlement status used to determine access to Pro.
  • Technical and connection information associated with purchase services, such as app version, device and operating-system information, and IP address.

The developer can view purchase-related records and entitlement status through RevenueCat. Google Play handles payment information and Google account information under its own policies. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

The App does not send your saved workout names, combinations or generated workout audio to RevenueCat.

For more information about these providers, see Google's Privacy Policy and RevenueCat's Privacy Policy. Google Play purchase and refund processes are subject to Google's policies and any applicable consumer rights.

7. Data Sharing and Website Access

We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising. Google Play and RevenueCat process purchase-related information to provide the services described above.

When you open our public privacy-policy page or retrieve hosted app assets, the hosting provider may process ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and request details, to deliver and secure those resources. The App does not attach saved workout content to these requests.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored workout information remains on your device until you delete it in the App, clear app storage, uninstall the App or otherwise manage it through Android, subject to device backup and restore settings.

Clearing app data or uninstalling the App does not automatically delete purchase records held by Google Play or RevenueCat. Purchase-related records may be retained to support purchase restoration, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or meet legal obligations, subject to the providers' applicable policies and legal requirements.

If you contact us, we retain correspondence only as needed to respond, resolve the matter and satisfy any applicable legal requirements.

For questions or requests about purchase-related data or correspondence, contact us using the email address below. We will explain what information we can identify and delete, any necessary verification, and any retention that is required. Requests concerning information controlled by Google may also need to be made directly to Google.

Deleting data does not itself request a refund or guarantee that a purchase can subsequently be restored.

9. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App's purchase services or by contacting us, please contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action with the relevant service provider where necessary.

10. Security

We aim to minimise privacy risks by avoiding unnecessary collection and transmission of information and keeping workout content in app-private device storage. Purchase-related information is transmitted through the purchase providers' secure connections.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Keep your Android device and operating system updated and protect access to your device and Google account.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's functionality, data practices or applicable requirements change. An updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
