Privacy Policy

Boxing Combo Creator
Effective Date: 18 November 2026

1. Introduction

Boxing Combo Creator ("the App") is an Android application designed to help users practise boxing combinations through generated training sequences and spoken audio instructions.

We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how the App handles information.

2. Information Collection

The App does not require registration or the creation of a user account.

The App does not collect, transmit or store personal information on developer-operated servers.

We do not collect names, email addresses, precise location information, contacts, advertising identifiers or personal usage analytics through the App.

3. Audio Instructions

Boxing Combo Creator uses pre-generated audio files bundled with the application to provide spoken boxing instructions.

These audio files are played locally on the user's device. The App does not transmit users' voices, microphone recordings or training instructions to an online text-to-speech service.

4. Analytics and Advertising

The App does not display advertisements and does not integrate advertising tracking services.

We do not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting services to collect information about users.

5. Local Device Storage

The App may store application preferences, settings or training-related information locally on the user's device.

This information is used to support application functionality and is not transmitted to developer-operated servers.

Users may remove locally stored application data using their device's application storage settings or by uninstalling the App, subject to the device's backup and restore settings.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share users' personal information with advertisers, data brokers or other third parties.

Except for Google Play purchase processing described below, the App does not transmit personal information to developer-operated servers or third-party services.

In-App Purchases and Google Play

Boxing Combo Creator is free to download and offers an optional one-time in-app purchase to unlock paid features. The price is shown in Google Play before you confirm the purchase.

Google Play processes payments and may collect and use information such as your Google account details, payment information and transaction history in accordance with its own privacy policy. We do not receive or store your full payment card details. Google Play may provide the App with purchase and entitlement information to help determine access to paid features.

For information about how Google handles data, visit Google’s Privacy Policy. Purchase records and refund requests are handled through Google Play, subject to its policies.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not maintain user accounts or retain personal user information on developer-operated servers.

Any locally stored application information remains on the user's device until it is cleared, removed or otherwise managed through the device's operating system.

For questions regarding data deletion, users may contact us using the email address below.

8. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

If you have concerns regarding children's privacy, please contact us.

9. Security

We aim to minimise privacy risks by avoiding unnecessary collection and transmission of personal information.

Users should keep their Android devices and operating systems updated to maintain device security.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's functionality, data practices or applicable requirements change.

Any updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
